The Story

Built by a BrSE,
for the whole team

Working as a BrSE/PM at a Japanese product company, I watched information get lost in translation chains — IT Comtors, BAs, BrSEs all passing context along until the original meaning was gone, costing hours of extra meetings.

With the spirit of "工夫" — always finding a way to improve — I built Viezan: a tool that lets everyone communicate directly with AI translation, no intermediary needed.

The app is local-first. It can call local runtimes directly, or provider APIs with your own keys. No Viezan relay server sits between you and the provider you choose.
